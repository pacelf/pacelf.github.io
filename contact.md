      Some documents need to be scanned before a digital copy can be made available.

      1. Find the document in the [library](../library/).
      2. Click the email icon in the document title bar.
      3. Send the email with the document ID and title already filled in.

      You can also email **pacelf@jcu.edu.au** directly. Please include the **document ID and title**.

      **[Email a scan request](mailto:pacelf@jcu.edu.au?subject=Document%20scan%20request)**

    content: |
      Looking for documents relevant to your research, or have another question about the PacELF library? Email us at **pacelf@jcu.edu.au**.

      Tell us about your research topic and what you're looking for. It helps to include any countries, time periods, or subjects of interest, along with details of documents you've already found.

      **[Ask for library assistance](mailto:pacelf@jcu.edu.au?subject=PacELF%20library%20assistance)**
